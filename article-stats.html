Article stats

For a goof, can we bring all of our publication metrics to one place?

For a goof? Why not.

Here you’ll find data logged from a collection of custom-built scrapers that fetch stats from Dimensions, OpenAlex and journal- or server-specific metrics (e.g. stats kept on Springer Nature, Frontiers journals, preprint or data repo servers, etc.) to display them all neatly in one graph. Sorted new-old (top-bottom). Refreshes every 48 hours. How convenient!
